---
title: Join a meeting
description: How participants join and tap their engagement.
---

1. Open the link from the invite, or scan the QR code.
2. Tap **I am speaking** or **This is interesting**. Tapping an active card again clears it. Your last state in a minute counts.
3. Leave whenever you like. Closing the page is fine.

You stay anonymous. BSBox stores a random token in your browser, never your name or email. See [Privacy](/en/privacy/).

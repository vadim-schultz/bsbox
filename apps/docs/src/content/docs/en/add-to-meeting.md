---
title: Add BSBox to a meeting
description: Insert a BSBox link into an Outlook invite or a Teams meeting.
---

## In Outlook

1. Open a new or existing meeting invite.
2. Open the BSBox add-in from the ribbon.
3. Choose **Insert BSBox link**. The invite now contains a join link and a QR code.
4. Send the invite as usual. For recurring meetings every occurrence gets its own results.

## In Teams

1. Open the meeting and select **BSBox** in the meeting side panel.
2. Share the join link in the chat, or show the QR code on screen.

Participants then [join the meeting](/en/join-meeting/).

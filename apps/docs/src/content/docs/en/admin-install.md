## Microsoft 365 admin center (Outlook)

1. Sign in to the Microsoft 365 admin center as a Global or Exchange administrator.
2. Go to **Settings > Integrated apps > Upload custom apps**.
3. Choose **Office Add-in** and upload the BSBox manifest.
4. Assign it to users or groups and confirm.

## Teams custom app

1. Open the Teams admin center and go to **Teams apps > Manage apps**.
2. Select **Upload new app** and upload the BSBox app package.
3. In **Setup policies**, allow the app for the intended users.

Changes can take up to 24 hours to appear. Problems? See [Support](/en/support/) and [Security](/en/security/).

---
title: Get started
description: What BSBox is and how to try it.
---

BSBox runs as an Outlook and Teams add-in. Participants join through a link, with no login and no account.

1. [Add BSBox to a meeting](/en/add-to-meeting/) as the organizer.
2. Share the invite. Participants [join the meeting](/en/join-meeting/) from any phone or browser.
3. After the meeting, [understand your score](/en/understand-score/).

Tenant administrators install the add-in once: see [Admin install](/en/admin-install/).

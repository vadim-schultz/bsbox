## What we collect

BSBox collects no personally identifiable information. Participants are identified by an anonymous token generated for each session. We store aggregate engagement counts per minute, a session code, and meeting timing.

## Retention

Session aggregates are deleted after 30 days. Live session data is purged 24 hours after a meeting ends.

## Where data lives

Data is processed on Cloudflare infrastructure. Cloudflare is our only subprocessor. The controller is established in the European Union and EU law applies.

## Your rights

Because no personal data is stored, we cannot link data to you. Contact us through [Support](/en/support/) with any request. See also [Terms](/en/terms/) and [Security](/en/security/).
